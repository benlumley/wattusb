Live wattage in your menu bar

wattusb

See exactly how many watts your Mac is pulling.

A tiny macOS menu bar app for USB-C charging power. Instantly tell whether a charger, or which port on a multi-port charger, is actually giving you good juice, and see where every watt goes.

Under the hood

How it works

All data comes straight from macOS's AppleSmartBattery IOKit service.

  • Live input draw: PowerTelemetryData.SystemPowerIn, the watts flowing in from the adapter.
  • PD contract: AdapterDetails gives the voltage/current the charger negotiated as its max.
  • Battery side: Voltage × Amperage reveals how much is actually reaching the cells.
  • The Mac itself: PowerTelemetryData.SystemLoad, whichever way the power's coming in.
  • Your devices: PowerOutDetails reports the watts going out of each USB-C port.
  • Refresh: every 2 seconds, kept for 5 minutes for the chart, drawn with Swift Charts.
